# Status

Slices 01-16 complete: a gin+templ+GORM framework whose public API (New, Wrap, Bind, Stack, Chain) stands over a db package. The demo app (cmd/sheidan) serves a templ home page, a reactive notes list and detail page driven by a GopherJS client (store, scheduler, minimal-diff reconciler), and note create/read/update/delete. The default middleware chain is twenty-one named slots, each with a lane taken from the name prefix, a fixed ordinal, and a kind. Builders shape chains with Enable, Disable, Configure, Insert, and Remove, rejecting malformed operations at compile time, and Chain produces per-group filtered copies.

Slots 1-2 are seated: request.id stamps or propagates an identifier, and logging.slog writes outcome-graded records (info, warn, error) bearing it, staying silent for preflight and static, web, and debug traffic. A guarded recovery ahead of the chain records panics through the same writer. The demo's pprof group is dark by default and switches on with SHEIDAN_DEBUG, optionally guarded by SHEIDAN_DEBUG_TOKEN.

Slice 16 filled the request-entry lanes (ordinals 3-10), so abusive or malformed traffic dies before it spends database or session work. gate.maintenance (3) answers 503 with a Retry-After hint while engaged, keeping /healthz and /static/ live and stamping admitted paths with an intake-bypass flag. gate.badiptarget (4) blocks listed IPs and networks, normalizing IPv6-mapped forms and warning loudly on private ranges. api.cors (7) ships closed (no origins) and grants explicitly, refusing the wildcard-plus-credentials pairing at build time. intake.postsize (8) caps bodies against a per-path table, honoring a declared length fast and clamping chunked transfers through a metering reader that yields an honest 413. intake.requestheaders (9) bounds the header block (line count, per-value length, aggregate bytes) and bans control sequences, answering 431 naming the breached dimension. intake.strings.trim (10) trims whitespace from string-typed inputs across JSON bodies, form fields, and query parameters, keeping excluded fields (passwords, keys) verbatim and parking the raw body for auditors. The guards stand down for bypass-stamped requests, so maintenance probes never trip them.

webbuild/ self-bootstraps GopherJS: it provisions a Go 1.21.13 SDK into the user cache on demand and builds the gopherjs CLI with Go 1.21-1.26 (CLI binaries made with newer toolchains panic on the js package). The transpiled web/web.js is gitignored and served by the demo.

Next (slices 17-21), filling the remaining lanes in order: sessions and cookie finalization (session.restore, resp.cookiefinalize, session.flash_errors). CSRF and signed requests (safety.csrf, safety.signature). Rate limiting, authorization, and deadline-bounded responses (policy.rate_limit, policy.authorization, resp.timeout). Localized views and substitution bindings (lang.localized, binding.params.subst), plus response caching (cache.response). Static serving and graceful shutdown.
